# Solutions Marketplace Video Pitch

## Evaluator mindset
Three SMEs, no stated requirement, five minutes of video, a weighted rubric, and a
stack of other companies' videos that day. They are not asking "does this meet the
need." They are asking "is this a real, credible thing an IC customer could buy
without re-competing." Ambiguity reads as immaturity. A pitch that is vivid about the
product and vague about the problem loses the 15% problem-definition row and usually
the 45% technical-merit row with it, because innovation is scored *relative to the
state of the art you defined*.

## Required artifacts
- **Video script** timed to the rubric weighting, covering all four required content
  elements in order: problem, technical approach, mission impact, pricing model.
- **Video** produced to the stated format spec exactly (length, resolution, container,
  color space, no restrictive markings).
- **Slide package** — the prescribed chart count, in the Government's template, no
  additional slides.
- **Submission form content** — title, keywords, abstract, TRL, focus area, business
  size, FOCI, FedRAMP/ATO/IL/FCL posture, POCs.
- **Pricing model narrative** (`reference/pricing-artifacts/` — commercial pricing
  basis, funding structure, award type; no rates).
- **Marking/release review** — a specific gate confirming nothing proprietary,
  export-controlled, or CUI is in the video, the slides, or the form.

## Common pitfalls
- Treating the slide package as the deliverable and the video as a formality. The
  rubric is scored off the submission but the content requirements are written at the
  video. Anything that only appears on a slide is at risk of not being scored.
- Skipping "current state of the art." Half of the 15% problem row is explicitly the
  state of the art in the field of potential solutions. Most vendor videos skip it.
- Leaving the pricing row thin. It is 10% and it is the cheapest full-credit row in
  federal proposal work: three sentences, clearly stated, no numbers required.
- Claiming an authorization the company does not hold on the form fields (FedRAMP,
  ATO, IL). These are drop-downs and they are checked.
- Putting a restrictive marking, distribution statement, or proprietary legend on the
  slides out of habit. That is an automatic non-compliance return.
- Burning the five minutes on capability breadth. One solution, one problem, one
  advance in the state of the art.
