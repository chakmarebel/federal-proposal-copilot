# Pseudo-cleft

**Failure mode.** 'What does not vary is that...' is a cleft that withholds the point behind a copula. Say the invariant directly.

## Before (AI proposalese)

> Two officers may phrase an assessment differently. What does not vary is that the product leads with the decision, follows the required format, and traces material statements to the staff's own input.

## After (approved rewrite)

> Two officers may phrase an assessment differently; every product still leads with the decision, follows the required format, and traces material statements to the staff's own input.
