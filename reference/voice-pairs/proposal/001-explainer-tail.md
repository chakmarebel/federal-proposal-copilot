# Trailing explainer clause

**Failure mode.** The claim is made, then a ', which is...' tail annotates its significance. Reads as the writer narrating why the fact matters instead of stating what the actor does.

## Before (AI proposalese)

> The architecture is aligned to the Risk Management Framework (RMF) and handles sensitive-but-unclassified content, which is the classification level of the staff products this Challenge targets.

## After (approved rewrite)

> [Your Company] is designed for secure and classified environments and for accreditation under the Risk Management Framework (RMF). The proposed configuration supports the sensitive-but-unclassified staff content of this prototype.
