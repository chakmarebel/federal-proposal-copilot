# Overloaded sentence with stapled benefit tail

**Failure mode.** One sentence carries capability + compatibility + three benefits, glued with 'so'. Split into short declaratives; give the benefit its own actor.

## Before (AI proposalese)

> It is portable across supported Intel, AMD, and NVIDIA configurations and runs on the laptops and workstations the Army already fields, so fielding requires no hardware purchase, no new equipment authorization, and no wait on a procurement cycle.

## After (approved rewrite)

> [Your Company] is hardware- and operating-system-agnostic. It runs locally on Army-owned mobile devices, laptops, and workstations across supported Intel, AMD, and NVIDIA configurations. The Army deploys it on existing equipment without a separate hardware procurement.
